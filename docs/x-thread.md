## 2 / 5

How it works:

Open an event on your token → share the link → followers register from their
phone → draw winners and pay.

Registering costs them nothing. No COOK, no gas, no signature.

---

## 3 / 5

A giveaway where the host picks a winner and nobody can check is worth
nothing.

So the draw commits to a secret seed, then takes its randomness from a Cookie
Chain block that doesn't exist yet.

Knowing the seed buys you nothing.

---

## 4 / 5

Commitment and result both go on chain as memo transactions.

Anyone can recompute the draw in their own browser.

It's not a VRF, and a verifiable draw doesn't make the entry list honest. It
makes the draw checkable, not the creator trustworthy.
